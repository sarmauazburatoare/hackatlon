## Separate current playground

The [Megazone Kids visitor page](https://www.megazonetimisoara.ro/megazone-kids), backed by [its public initial data](https://www.megazonetimisoara.ro/api/init), identifies **BEGA Lipovei, Strada Ion Ionescu de la Brad 15**, entrance between DM and Sinsay, **+40 774 937 102**. The visitor block (textnode 104, modified 2 September 2026) advertises children aged 0–8, an accompanying adult in the play area for under-4s, walk-ins with advance reservation advised, minute-based fees and free adult entry. Current published packages list 30 lei/child/hour and a 25 lei offer Monday–Thursday 10:00–16:00.

Its contact block gives 10:00–21:30 daily, while footer/telephone/booking copy gives 10:00–22:00. This conflict is preserved, not resolved by assumption. No booking was made, and published slots are not real-time capacity confirmation.

This is direct evidence for a **separately named, separately addressed Megazone Kids venue**. No inspected primary statement says it is a relocated Paradisul Piraților or Tunurile Piraților. Current Megazone terms content also contains placeholder text, so it cannot resolve legal succession.

## Interpretation and remaining gap

The redirect, retained old Facebook link, updated Lipovei contacts and mixed terms strongly support a **commercial web migration to Megazone Bites**. This is an inference about the website/brands. It does not establish that the same legal entity operates both, that the student-complex venue closed or moved, or that its original children’s facilities remain available.

A dated operator statement or comparable primary evidence is still needed for the original site’s current restaurant/playground status and admission. No primary corporate-register extract establishing succession was obtained. The public Facebook page was blocked during retrieval.
